Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, phone number, email address, case details, injury information, accident details, message content, SMS consent status, and any other information you submit through website forms, phone calls, emails, text messages, or other communications.

We may also automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you visit the website, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, referral source, approximate location, advertising source, campaign attribution data, call tracking data, cookies, pixels, analytics data, and similar website usage information.

Some information submitted through the website or intake process may be considered sensitive personal information, including injury details, accident details, health-related information, and other information related to a potential legal claim.

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential case review requests, contact you by phone, email, or text message, provide intake follow-up, schedule consultations, operate and improve the website, measure marketing performance, track advertising attribution, improve advertising campaigns, protect website security, comply with legal obligations, and support internal business operations.

Third-Party Services and Vendors

We may use third-party service providers to help operate the website, manage intake, track phone calls, analyze website activity, deliver advertising, measure marketing performance, and support business operations. These providers may include website hosting providers, CallRail or other call tracking providers, Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), Google Ads, Meta/Facebook advertising tools, Reddit, Taboola, CRM or intake software, email and SMS communication platforms, and other service providers.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising Technologies

The website may use cookies, pixels, tags, call tracking, analytics tools, and advertising technologies to understand how visitors use the site, measure advertising performance, improve user experience, and support marketing and remarketing efforts. These tools may collect information about your browser, device, IP address, pages visited, referral source, and interactions with the website.

For more detail on which technologies are used and how to manage your choices, see our Cookie Policy.

SMS and Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, we may send SMS messages related to your inquiry, case review request, appointment reminders, intake follow-up, or other communications. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not required as a condition of hiring a lawyer or requesting a case review.

Phone Calls and Call Tracking

Phone calls placed to or from numbers displayed on this website may be tracked for attribution, quality control, intake review, training, and marketing performance. Calls may also be recorded where permitted by law and with appropriate notice or consent. Call tracking is provided through CallRail or similar providers, which may dynamically swap displayed phone numbers based on the visitor’s marketing source.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information with service providers, vendors, advertising and analytics partners, intake providers, co-counsel or referral counsel where appropriate, and other parties as needed to respond to your inquiry, evaluate your case review request, operate the website, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or as otherwise permitted by law.

Some advertising, analytics, retargeting, or lead-review activity may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under California law. California residents can opt out at Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.

Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, evaluate case review requests, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and support legitimate business purposes.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have certain rights regarding their personal information, including the right to request access to personal information, request deletion, request correction, opt out of certain sharing or sale of personal information where applicable, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information where applicable, and not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

For full detail, see our California Privacy Rights notice. To submit a privacy request, please contact us using the contact information listed on this website.

No Attorney-Client Relationship Through Website Submission

Submitting information through this website, calling, texting, or emailing does not automatically create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is only formed after a matter has been reviewed and both the user and the reviewing attorney or law firm have agreed in writing to representation.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no website, email, phone, or electronic communication system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit a privacy request, please contact us using the information below or through our Contact page.
