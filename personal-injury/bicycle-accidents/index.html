What a Free Claim Review Covers

A free claim review is a no-cost, no-commitment look at where a California injury claim actually stands. It does not require you to switch lawyers and it does not create an attorney-client relationship. A review typically covers:

  • Liability. How fault looks based on the facts, the police report, and any available evidence.
  • Insurance coverage. Primary, excess, umbrella, employer, and UM/UIM layers that may apply.
  • Damages. Medical bills paid and projected, lost wages, lost earning capacity, and pain and suffering framing.
  • Settlement posture. Whether the current offer (or the absence of one) is in line with how California carriers usually value this kind of claim.
  • Next step. Whether the situation calls for a second opinion, a settlement checklist review, an updated demand letter, or attorney involvement.
