Common California Injury Claim & Claim Review Questions

What is a free claim review?

A free claim review is a no-cost look at your California injury claim — the facts of the accident, the injuries, available insurance coverage, and whether the current direction (offer, demand, lawyer, or DIY claim) makes sense for you. It is meant to help you understand how injury claim value is calculated and review a settlement offer before accepting. It does not commit you to hiring anyone and submitting a form does not create an attorney-client relationship.

When should I get a second opinion on my injury claim?

Many people request a second opinion when an insurance company is pressuring them to accept a quick offer, when their current lawyer has stopped communicating, when a settlement is about to be signed, or when the explanation of "value" does not add up. You can get a second opinion on your injury claim without firing your current lawyer.

Should I accept the insurance company's first offer?

Often, no. First offers are commonly low and may not account for future medical care, lost earning capacity, medical liens, or pain and suffering. Before you accept, it is reasonable to review whether to accept the first offer and learn what happens after a demand letter is exchanged.

Do I have to hire a lawyer to use Insider Lawyers?

No. Insider Lawyers is an information and review resource. You can read our California Personal Injury Claim Guide, our California Settlement Guide, or use the free claim review for a neutral look at your situation. If your matter looks like it may need legal representation, you may be connected with a qualified attorney or legal team for further review — but it is always your choice whether to move forward.

Is the claim review confidential?

We handle submissions privately, but contacting Insider Lawyers does not create an attorney-client relationship or attorney-client privilege. Do not submit highly confidential or time-sensitive information until legal representation is confirmed.
