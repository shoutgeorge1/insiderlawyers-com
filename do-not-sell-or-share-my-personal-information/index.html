Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last Updated: June 11, 2026.

California residents may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information where applicable.

Insider Lawyers does not sell personal information for money. However, some privacy laws define “sale” or “sharing” broadly, and certain advertising, analytics, remarketing, or tracking technologies on our website may be considered a sale or sharing of personal information under applicable California law.

You may submit a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information by contacting us through the contact information listed on this website, by using the form below, or by enabling a recognized Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser. Please include your name, email address, phone number, and enough information for us to identify and process your request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.
