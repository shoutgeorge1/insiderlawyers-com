What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files that may be placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies can help websites function, remember preferences, understand visitor behavior, measure marketing performance, and improve user experience.

Cookies may be “first-party” (set by Insider Lawyers) or “third-party” (set by a service provider that helps us operate the website or run our advertising). They may be “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or “persistent” cookies (stored for a defined period).
